---
title: Bring your own app
description: Post through your own platform developer app.
---

You can plug your own developer app (client ID and secret) into PostPipe for each platform. Posts for that platform then go through your app.

## Why

There are two levels.

- An app of yours that the platform has approved lets your accounts and your users post publicly today, even on platforms PostPipe is not approved for yet.
- An app that is not approved yet posts only within the platform's test limits, mostly to your own accounts. See the table below.

Platforms PostPipe has not been approved for yet become available to your organisation once you add your app. Bluesky needs no app of your own.

## Steps

1. Create a developer app on the platform. Each platform page has an App setup section: [X](/docs/platforms/x), [LinkedIn](/docs/platforms/linkedin), [Facebook](/docs/platforms/facebook), [Instagram](/docs/platforms/instagram), [Threads](/docs/platforms/threads), [TikTok](/docs/platforms/tiktok), [YouTube](/docs/platforms/youtube), [Pinterest](/docs/platforms/pinterest).
2. In the dashboard open Settings, then Your own platform apps.
3. Paste the client ID and client secret on the platform's card, then press Save. For Facebook the Login config ID is required too.
4. Register the callback URL shown on the card in your app's settings on the platform.

Only the owner of the organisation can change these settings. The secret is stored encrypted and is never shown again.

## What an unapproved app can do

| Platform | Limit |
|---|---|
| X | Needs a funded X developer account (pay-per-use). |
| LinkedIn | Personal posts work self-serve. Company pages need LinkedIn Community Management approval. |
| Facebook | Without Meta App Review your app can post only for accounts that have a role on it. |
| Instagram | Without Meta App Review your app can post only for accounts that have a role on it. |
| Threads | Without Meta App Review your app can post only for accounts that have a role on it. |
| TikTok | Unaudited TikTok apps post only for their own test accounts, as drafts to the inbox. |
| YouTube | Google testing mode: up to 100 test users, private uploads, tokens expire after 7 days. |
| Pinterest | Pinterest trial access creates private pins only. |

## Things to know

- Accounts keep the app they were connected with. If you change or remove your app, accounts connected through it stop working. Reconnect them afterwards.
- Removing your app does not move existing accounts to PostPipe's app.
- Pinterest: `PINTEREST_ENV` (sandbox or production) is set once for the whole deployment and applies to every organisation's Pinterest app.
- Posts through your own X app are not billed X usage by PostPipe; X bills your developer account directly.
